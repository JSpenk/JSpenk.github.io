Under the hood

What's real and what's simulated

59.19 Hz

Measured timing

I simulated the board's Verilog (Tankb_fpga.v) in Icarus Verilog and timed the outputs: 6 MHz pixel clock, 384 × 264 raster, 64 µs lines, 5.33 µs HSYNC, 8-line VSYNC, 256 × 224 visible. The game steps at exactly that frame rate.

9 lines

Fault bus

Each target is a signal that can be forced to 0 or 1, permanently or in random bursts. Control faults rewrite the input lines before the game reads them, and video faults act on the display stage.

Original

Game & art

The tank game, sprites and sound are original code written for this page. The arcade ROMs are Namco's, so they are not included. The display behavior is modeled on the real hardware's timing, not copied from it.
